Bots Now Outnumber Humans: Build Your Site for Both
Over half of all web traffic is now automated, and AI agents like ChatGPT Atlas and Claude Computer Use are reading your site in three ways: screenshots, accessibility trees, and raw HTML. If your pages rely on JavaScript rendering or skip semantic markup, these agents may simply not understand you. Semantic HTML and server-rendered content are no longer best practices; they are survival tactics.

02 🔬 GEO Research 5 min read
Branded Prompts Get 18x More AI Mentions. Here Is Why.
Moz ran 300 prompts through Gemini and found a striking gap: branded queries triggered 14.5 brand mentions on average, while generic ones averaged just 0.79. The takeaway for marketers is clear: the prompts you track shape what you see, and most brand monitoring programs are dangerously narrow. Diversify your prompt spectrum or risk measuring a mirage.
